Scrum organises work in fixed-length sprints of one to four weeks, with a product owner, a scrum master, and a self-organising team. A backlog is refined continuously and each sprint ends in a potentially shippable increment and a review. It fits software with changing requirements, a reachable product owner, and a small co-located or well-connected team, such as infotainment apps. It fails when hardware lead times, supplier milestones, or heavy safety evidence dominate, because sprint rhythm cannot absorb long external dependencies. Typical roles: product owner, scrum master, developers. Deliverables: working increments and a backlog. In automotive it is often combined with a V-shaped frame for safety-relevant work.
