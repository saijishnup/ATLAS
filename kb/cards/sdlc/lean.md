Lean software development applies lean-manufacturing ideas: remove waste, amplify learning, decide late, deliver fast, build quality in, and optimise the whole flow. It focuses on value streams and avoiding unneeded features or handoffs. It fits mature teams that want to cut delay and rework in an existing delivery pipeline, such as a connected-services backend. It fails as a standalone method for a new safety-critical project because it gives principles rather than concrete phases, gates, or evidence rules. Typical roles: cross-functional team and a value-stream owner. Deliverables: reduced lead time and fewer defects. In automotive it often complements agile or DevOps practice.
