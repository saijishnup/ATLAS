Big Bang development starts coding with little or no planning, specification, or process, and the product takes shape as effort and money arrive. It is treated as an anti-pattern for real projects. It can suit tiny experiments, throwaway proofs of concept, or personal learning work where failure costs nothing and no one else depends on the result. It fails on almost everything else: requirements stay undefined, effort cannot be estimated, defects appear late, and there is no evidence trail. It is unsuitable for anything touching safety, regulation, or security, including any automotive production software. Roles are informal, often one developer. Deliverables are just the code. Included so the tool can explain why it ranks low and what to choose instead.
