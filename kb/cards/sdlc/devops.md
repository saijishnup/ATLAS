DevOps joins development and operations through automation: continuous integration, automated testing, infrastructure as code, monitoring, and fast feedback from running systems. It aims for frequent, low-risk releases and shared responsibility for production. It fits services that change often and run in the field, such as telematics backends and OTA delivery platforms. It fails when change needs long external approvals or when security and safety evidence are not built into the pipeline. Typical roles: developers, operations or platform engineers, quality engineers. Deliverables: pipelines, release automation, and monitoring. In automotive it extends to vehicle fleets through staged OTA rollout, with DevSecOps adding security controls.
