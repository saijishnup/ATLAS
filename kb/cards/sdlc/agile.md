Agile builds software in short iterations, delivering working increments and adapting plans from feedback. Requirements live in a prioritised backlog that changes continuously. It fits products with volatile requirements, available stakeholders, and low cost of failure per release, such as infotainment apps or companion mobile apps. Used alone it struggles when safety or regulatory evidence must be produced upfront, since light documentation and evolving scope weaken audit trails. In automotive, teams often add safety and cybersecurity activities into sprints, or combine agile with a V-shaped outer frame. Typical roles: product owner, developers, team lead, testers. Key practices: sprint planning, continuous integration, retrospectives.
