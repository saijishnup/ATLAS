MBSE-V uses a V-shaped lifecycle in which system and software requirements, architecture, and behaviour are held as formal models, for example in SysML or Simulink, rather than only as documents. Models are simulated early, code may be generated from them, and traceability follows the model. It fits complex, hardware-coupled, safety-critical systems such as battery management or ADAS functions, where early simulation reduces integration risk. It fails when the team lacks modelling skill and tooling, or when requirements change faster than models can be maintained. Typical roles: systems engineer, model architect, safety manager, verification engineers. Deliverables: system models, generated code, and model-based test evidence.
