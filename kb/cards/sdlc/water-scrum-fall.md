Water-Scrum-Fall is a pragmatic hybrid: upfront planning and requirements are done Waterfall-style, development runs in Scrum sprints, and integration, acceptance, and release follow a Waterfall-style gate sequence. It reflects how many large organisations really work. It fits projects where the business or contract demands fixed scope and milestones but the development team prefers short iterations, such as a supplier delivering to an OEM specification. It fails when the Waterfall ends hide late integration surprises, so agile speed in the middle is lost. Typical roles: project manager, product owner, scrum team, test and release teams. Deliverables: baselined specification, sprint increments, and release package.
