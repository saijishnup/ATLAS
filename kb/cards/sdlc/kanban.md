Kanban visualises work on a board, limits work in progress, and pulls items through stages without fixed iterations. Flow metrics such as cycle time guide improvement. It fits maintenance, support, and diagnostics-tool work where requests arrive unpredictably and priorities shift daily. It fails when a project needs planned milestones, formal release gates, or heavy upfront design, because the model has no built-in planning cadence. Typical roles: team members and a service or flow manager. Deliverables: continuous small releases and flow reports. In automotive it suits bug-fix streams, dealer-tool updates, and post-release support, and less so new safety-critical function development.
