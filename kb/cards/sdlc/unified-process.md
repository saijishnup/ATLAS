The Unified Process is an iterative, use-case-driven framework with four phases: inception, elaboration, construction, and transition. Architecture is stabilised early, then built in timeboxed iterations with risk reduction in each. It fits large, architecture-heavy projects with a stable team and a need for rich modelling artefacts, such as a vehicle gateway platform. It fails for small teams or fast-moving work because the process is heavy and documentation-oriented. Typical roles: project manager, architect, analysts, developers, testers. Deliverables: use-case model, architecture baseline, iteration builds, and test results. In automotive it appears in organisations that already use UML or SysML modelling and want iteration with architectural control.
