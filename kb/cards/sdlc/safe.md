SAFe scales agile across many teams using planning increments of roughly ten weeks, release trains, and shared architecture and portfolio layers. Teams plan together and integrate on a common cadence. It fits large programmes with dozens or hundreds of engineers, several suppliers, and a need to align agile teams with fixed vehicle milestones. It fails for small teams, where its roles and ceremonies add overhead, and it can hide weak engineering discipline behind process. Typical roles: release train engineer, product management, system architect, agile teams. Deliverables: aligned increments per planning cycle. In automotive it is used by OEMs and large suppliers to coordinate software-defined vehicle programmes.
