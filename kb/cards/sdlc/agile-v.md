Agile-V is a hybrid: a V-shaped frame supplies requirement levels, verification planning, and safety or compliance evidence, while teams work inside it in short iterations. Each increment is verified against its linked requirements, so evidence accumulates continuously instead of at the end. It fits automotive software with medium to high safety or regulatory demands but requirements that still evolve, such as ADAS features or software-defined vehicle functions. It fails when the team is undisciplined about traceability, because iteration speed then erodes the evidence trail. Typical roles: product owner, systems engineer, safety manager, developers, verification engineers. Deliverables: incremental releases plus updated traceability and test evidence.
