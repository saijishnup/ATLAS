The Spiral model repeats cycles of planning, risk analysis, engineering, and evaluation. Each loop builds a more complete version and tackles the biggest remaining risk first, often through prototypes. It fits projects with high technical uncertainty or new technology, for example a first-of-kind sensor fusion function where feasibility is unknown. It fails when the budget is tight or the team lacks risk-assessment skill, because the risk analysis itself adds cost and needs experienced judgement. Fixed-scope, fixed-price contracts fit it poorly. Typical roles: project manager, risk lead, architect, developers, customer representative. Deliverables per loop: updated risk register, prototype or increment, review decision to continue.
