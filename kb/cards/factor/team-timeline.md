Team size and timeline pressure shape how much coordination a lifecycle needs. A team of one to five people can work with light process and short cycles. Above about twenty people, coordination, shared architecture, and common planning become necessary. A tight fixed deadline reduces room for exploration and rework, which favours incremental delivery of the most important scope first. A flexible deadline allows iterative learning. Ask how many people work on the code and whether the delivery date is fixed. Effect on scoring: raises the fit of scaled and incremental models as size and pressure grow.
