Regulatory audit burden is how much external assessment the project must pass, from internal review only, to customer or supplier audits, to type approval or formal assessment. Heavy audit needs documented processes, baselines, traceability, and approvals at defined gates. Models that produce evidence as a by-product, such as V-Model or Agile-V, fit better than models that rely on informal knowledge. Ask which regulations or assessments apply and who signs off. Effect on scoring: raises the fit of gated and documentation-rich models as burden rises.
