Requirement volatility describes how often needs are expected to change during the project. Stable requirements, such as a fixed supplier specification, suit plan-driven models where design is completed before building. Volatile requirements, such as a new user-facing feature with unclear demand, suit iterative and agile models that welcome change. Sources of change include customer feedback, regulation updates, and hardware revisions. High volatility combined with high safety criticality is the hard case and points to hybrids such as Agile-V. Ask how many major changes are expected and how late they can arrive. Effect on scoring: raises the fit of iterative models when volatility is high, and of Waterfall and V-Model when it is low.
