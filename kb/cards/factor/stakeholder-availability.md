Stakeholder availability is how easily the people who decide requirements can be reached. Agile methods rely on frequent feedback from a product owner or customer. If stakeholders are hard to reach, or decisions happen only at formal reviews, short iterations stall and plan-driven models with upfront specification work better. Daily access supports sprints and an on-site customer, while weekly access suits iterative models with periodic reviews. Ask who approves requirements and how quickly they respond. Effect on scoring: raises the fit of agile-family models as availability rises.
