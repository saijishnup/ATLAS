Supplier and integration complexity is how many external parties deliver parts of the system and how complicated the interfaces are. With many suppliers, work depends on contracts, interface specifications, and shared milestones, and responsibility for safety and cybersecurity evidence must be agreed in writing. Late integration is the main risk. Plan-driven and V-shaped models, or scaled frameworks with common planning cycles, fit better than a single small agile team. Ask who owns each interface and how often suppliers deliver. Effect on scoring: raises the fit of plan-driven, SAFe, and Water-Scrum-Fall models as complexity rises.
