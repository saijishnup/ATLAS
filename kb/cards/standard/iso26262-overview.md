ISO 26262 covers functional safety of electrical and electronic systems in road vehicles. It defines a safety lifecycle from concept to decommissioning, with hazard analysis, safety goals, a functional and technical safety concept, hardware and software development, and verification and validation. Each phase has work products and reviews, and the rigour depends on the ASIL assigned to the function. The standard expects planning, configuration management, change management, and confirmation measures such as independent reviews and safety audits. For a project it drives traceability from hazards through safety requirements to tests, and favours lifecycles that produce this evidence reliably. Summary only: verify against the standard.
