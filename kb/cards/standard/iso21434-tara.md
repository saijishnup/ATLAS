Threat analysis and risk assessment, or TARA, identifies assets, damage scenarios, threat scenarios, and attack paths, then rates impact and attack feasibility to give a risk value. Risk treatment options include avoiding, reducing, sharing, or retaining the risk. Outcomes are cybersecurity goals and claims that feed the concept and requirements. TARA is repeated when the design, threats, or vulnerabilities change. For requirements work, each security requirement should link to a threat scenario or cybersecurity goal, so the reason for it is visible and testable. Summary only: verify against the standard.
