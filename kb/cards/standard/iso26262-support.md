Part 8 covers processes that support all lifecycle phases: management of safety requirements, configuration management, change management, verification, documentation, qualification of software tools and components, and proven-in-use arguments. Tools that could introduce or fail to detect errors need a confidence assessment based on the tool's use case and the chance of detection. Changes must be assessed for impact on safety before release. These ideas matter for fast iteration, because frequent changes still need controlled baselines, impact analysis, and recorded evidence.
