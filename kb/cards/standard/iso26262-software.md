Part 6 sets requirements for product development at the software level. It asks for software safety requirements derived from the technical safety concept, a software architectural design, unit design and implementation, then unit testing, integration testing, and verification against requirements. It recommends methods by ASIL, such as defensive programming, coding guidelines, static analysis, requirement-based tests, and structural coverage measures with stricter targets at higher ASIL. Freedom from interference between software of different integrity levels must be shown. Safety requirements need verifiable acceptance criteria and bidirectional traceability to design and tests.
