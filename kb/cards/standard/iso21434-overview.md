ISO/SAE 21434 defines cybersecurity engineering for road vehicle electrical and electronic systems across the full lifecycle. It covers organisational cybersecurity management, project-dependent activities including a cybersecurity case, concept, product development, validation, production, operations and maintenance, and end of support. It expects a cybersecurity plan, defined roles, and work products with traceability from goals to requirements and tests. The standard is process oriented, so organisations are assessed on how they work as well as on product features. It is widely used as the practical route to meet the R155 management-system expectations. Summary only: verify against the standard.
