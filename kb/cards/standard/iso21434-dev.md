After concept, cybersecurity goals are refined into cybersecurity requirements, a design, and an implementation. Verification uses reviews, vulnerability analysis, and testing such as fuzzing and penetration testing. Integration and verification confirm that requirements are met, and validation confirms that cybersecurity goals are achieved on the item. Vulnerabilities found during development are analysed and managed. The standard expects traceability between goals, requirements, design, and tests. This evidence can be produced in short cycles, if each increment keeps its links and records. Summary only: verify against the standard.
