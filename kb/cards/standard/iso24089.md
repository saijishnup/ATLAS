ISO 24089 gives engineering requirements and recommendations for software updates of road vehicles. It covers organisation-level processes and project-level activities for planning, preparing, and executing updates, including update packages, campaigns, and the vehicle-side update mechanism. It supports the process evidence expected under UNECE R156 and links to cybersecurity engineering under ISO/SAE 21434. Topics include identifying affected software, checking compatibility, safe execution, rollback, and recording what was updated. For an update project it is a source for process requirements beyond the regulation's wording. Summary only: verify against the standard.
