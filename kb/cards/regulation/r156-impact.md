Before an update is issued, the manufacturer must identify interdependencies with other systems, identify target vehicles, and confirm that the update is compatible with each target's last known software and hardware configuration. It must also assess and record whether the update affects type-approved systems, adds or changes functions, or affects other systems needed for safe operation. For each update the records cover purpose, affected functions, approval status, execution conditions, and confirmation of successful verification and validation. Update requirements should ensure this information can be produced and stored.
