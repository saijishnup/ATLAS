Annex 5 gives the baseline list of threats and mitigations. Part A groups threats such as back-end servers, vehicle communication channels, update procedures, unintended human actions, external connectivity, vehicle data and code, and weaknesses that remain if systems are not hardened. Part B lists mitigations for the vehicle, for example verifying authenticity and integrity of received messages, secure software update procedures, protecting cryptographic keys, access control, and security controls on external interfaces such as USB and the OBD port. Part C covers mitigations outside the vehicle, such as back-end systems. Possible impacts include effects on safe operation and breaches of data integrity or confidentiality.
