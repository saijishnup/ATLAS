The CSMS must include processes to identify risks to the vehicle type, assess and categorise them, treat them, and verify they are managed. The threats listed in Annex 5 Part A must be considered, along with any other relevant threats. For the vehicle type, the manufacturer identifies critical elements and performs an exhaustive risk assessment covering the elements, their interactions, and external systems. Requirements derived from a threat analysis should therefore trace back to a documented risk. A requirement that only says the system shall be secure, with no linked threat or risk, is weak evidence for approval.
