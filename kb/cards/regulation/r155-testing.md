The manufacturer must have processes for testing the cybersecurity of a vehicle type, and must perform appropriate and sufficient testing before type approval to verify that the implemented security measures are effective. The approval authority can also test a sample vehicle, with a focus on risks assessed as high. Each security requirement should therefore have a testable acceptance criterion and a place in the verification plan, for example penetration testing or fuzzing of external interfaces. Test results are part of the documentation package presented for approval.
