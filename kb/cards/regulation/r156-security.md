The authenticity and integrity of software updates must be protected to reasonably prevent compromise and invalid updates. The manufacturer must show that updates are protected from manipulation before the update process starts, that the update process and the development of the delivery system are protected against compromise, and that verification and validation of vehicle software are appropriate. In practice this points to signed update packages, integrity checks on the vehicle, and a protected build and delivery pipeline. It overlaps with the R155 update-procedure mitigation.
