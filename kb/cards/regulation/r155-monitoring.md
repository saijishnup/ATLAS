The CSMS must monitor for, detect, and respond to cyber-attacks, threats, and vulnerabilities on vehicle types, including vehicles after first registration. Threats that need a response must be mitigated within a reasonable timeframe. Monitoring from vehicle data and logs must respect privacy rights, especially consent. The manufacturer reports monitoring outcomes to the approval authority at least once a year and confirms that mitigations remain effective. Software projects need logging, secure data collection, incident response, and a way to ship fixes, often by over-the-air update, after launch.
