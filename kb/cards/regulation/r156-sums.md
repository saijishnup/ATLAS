The regulation applies to vehicles that permit software updates. The manufacturer needs a Software Update Management System, or SUMS, meaning a systematic approach with organisational processes and procedures for delivering updates. A Certificate of Compliance for the SUMS is issued after assessment, is valid for at most three years, and is a documentation requirement for type approval. Update capability is therefore a regulated process, not just a feature. Requirements for an update client or back end should support the manufacturer's ability to show these processes to an approval authority.
