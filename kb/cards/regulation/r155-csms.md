The regulation makes a Cyber Security Management System, or CSMS, a condition of vehicle type approval. The approval authority checks that the vehicle manufacturer has such a system and that it works. The CSMS must cover three phases: development, production, and post-production, so cybersecurity duties continue after the vehicle is sold. The manufacturer holds a Certificate of Compliance for the CSMS, valid for up to three years and renewable. For a software project this means cybersecurity requirements are not a one-time design task. They need organisational processes, named responsibilities, and evidence across the whole vehicle lifetime.
