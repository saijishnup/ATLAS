The manufacturer needs a process to uniquely identify all initial and updated software versions, with integrity validation data, and the related hardware of a type-approved system. Where a vehicle type uses an RXSWIN, each one must be uniquely identifiable and updated when a change leads to a type-approval extension or new approval. For every RXSWIN there is an auditable register of relevant software before and after an update. The RXSWIN or software version must be readable through a standard interface, at least the OBD port, and protected against unauthorised modification.
