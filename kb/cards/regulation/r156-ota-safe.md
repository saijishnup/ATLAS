For updates over the air, the manufacturer must show how it assesses that an update will not affect safety if conducted while driving. The vehicle must be able to restore the previous version after a failed or interrupted update, or be placed in a safe state. Updates may run only when the vehicle has enough power to finish, including any recovery. Where execution may be unsafe while driving, the manufacturer must show how it prevents driving and the use of safety-affecting functions during the update. Preconditions must be met before execution begins.
