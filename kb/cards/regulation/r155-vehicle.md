For the vehicle type, the manufacturer must protect against the risks found in its assessment with proportionate mitigations, including the relevant mitigations from Annex 5 Parts B and C or an equally appropriate alternative. The vehicle must be able to detect and prevent cyber-attacks, support the manufacturer's monitoring, and provide data forensic capability. Cryptographic modules must follow consensus standards, or the choice must be justified. These become design requirements for authentication, intrusion detection, secure logging, and standard cryptography in the vehicle software.
