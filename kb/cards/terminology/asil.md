ASIL stands for Automotive Safety Integrity Level, a rating from A (lowest) to D (highest) used in functional-safety work. A hazard analysis rates each hazard by the severity of harm, how often the situation occurs, and how controllable it is for the driver. The combination gives the level, or QM when the risk is low enough that only normal quality management applies. The level sets how strict development and verification must be, including test coverage, reviewer independence, and documentation. Requirements inherit the level of the function they protect and can be decomposed to lower levels under defined rules. Summary only: verify against the standard for any real project.
