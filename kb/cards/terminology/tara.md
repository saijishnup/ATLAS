TARA is the cybersecurity counterpart to hazard analysis. The team lists assets worth protecting, imagines damage that could happen, describes threat scenarios and attack paths, and rates impact and how feasible each attack is. The result is a risk value used to decide treatment and to set cybersecurity goals. It is repeated when design or threat knowledge changes. In requirement work it supplies the reason behind security requirements.
