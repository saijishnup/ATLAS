A Cyber Security Management System is a systematic, risk-based approach that defines organisational processes, responsibilities, and governance to treat risk from cyber threats to vehicles and protect them from cyber-attacks. Under R155 the manufacturer needs a valid certificate for it before a vehicle type can be approved. It covers development, production, and post-production phases.
