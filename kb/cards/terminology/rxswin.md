An RXSWIN is a dedicated identifier, defined by the vehicle manufacturer, for the type-approval-relevant software of an electronic control system. It lets the manufacturer and authorities tell which software configuration a vehicle carries. R156 sets rules for its uniqueness, readability through a standard interface, and protection when a vehicle type uses one.
