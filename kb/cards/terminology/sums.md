A Software Update Management System is a systematic approach defining organisational processes and procedures to meet the regulation's requirements for delivering software updates. It includes identifying software versions, checking target vehicle compatibility, assessing effect on type approval, and keeping records. The manufacturer obtains a certificate of compliance for it.
